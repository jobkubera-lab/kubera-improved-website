# Morden Hall Park Digital Discovery Trail

An independent, mobile-first concept for a location-aware visitor trail at Morden Hall Park.

## Purpose

The prototype demonstrates how small QR markers could connect visitors to concise stories about the park's wildlife, industrial heritage, river, trees and Rose Garden without requiring an app download.

## Pilot concept

- Three removable QR markers
- Wetland Boardwalk, Snuff Mill and Rose Garden
- Short visual stories and 60-second audio prompts
- 15- and 30-minute route choices
- Step-free and family route options
- Multilingual and accessible content
- Twelve-week trial with anonymous usage measurement

## Status

This is an independent proposal prototype. It is not an official National Trust product and does not claim endorsement by National Trust.

All final historical, ecological, accessibility, photographic and brand content would require review and written approval from National Trust before public use.
