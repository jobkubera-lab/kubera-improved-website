Ridge Interior Furnishings

Antique restoration

Project journey

Clear from first message to installation.

Better enquiries save the workshop time and make the client feel looked after from the beginning.

  1. 01

    Initial brief

    The client sends photographs, dimensions, drawings, location, intended use and required timing.

  2. 02

    Workshop review

    Ridge assesses structure, materials, fabric, finish, access requirements and whether a site visit is needed.

  3. 03

    Scope & quotation

    A clear scope records what is included, assumptions, client-supplied elements and the agreed programme.

  4. 04

    Making

    Frame, upholstery, sewing and finishing are coordinated within the workshop.

  5. 05

    Progress updates

    Selected process photographs keep designers and clients informed without creating unnecessary administration.

  6. 06

    Delivery & installation

    Final quality checks, protected delivery and installation coordination where required.
