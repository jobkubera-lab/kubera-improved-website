The process

Clear from first conversation to delivery.

  1. 01

    Brief

    Share photographs, dimensions, drawings, location, timing and the intended use of the piece.

  2. 02

    Scope

    Ridge reviews construction, materials, finish, practical constraints and the appropriate route forward.

  3. 03

    Making

    The workshop develops the frame, upholstery and details with progress documented for the client or design team.

  4. 04

    Delivery

    Final checks, coordinated delivery and installation where the project requires it.
